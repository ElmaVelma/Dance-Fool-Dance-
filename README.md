In this project, you play as a square, the fool, and you must go from left to right to collect the falling
items on the screen, aka dancing.
For every fallen item you collect you get 5 points for each but, 
for every item you miss it causes you to lose one life, with you having 5 lives in total.
You also have a personal high score you get for the most amount of items you collect.

- Explaining each feature -
Feature one:
The Start Screen,
    Its a simple three buttons, 
    space to begin the main game
    h to see your high score
    q to quit out of the game enitrely

Feature Two:
A simple countdown,
    This is a simple countdown from 3 to get the player ready to play the main game

Feature Three:
The Main Game Loop,
    This is where you are a square and move left to right, trying to catch falling blocks from the sky.
    As time goes on the blocks get more and more faster, making it more difficult to catch them.

Feature Four:
The lives,
    You get 5 lives in total during the game, if a block falls off screen you lose one life.
    Once you lose all 5 the game is over.

Feature Five:
The Death Screen,
    This is a game over menu, where it displays the words "Game Over" and your final score.
    You have the option to either press r to restart the game, or press q to quit out of it.

Lastly, Feature Six:
The Live numbers duirng the main game loop, 
    There are three categories, being "Score: ", "High Score: ", and "Lives: "
    The score will go up depending how how many falling items you collect.
    Your high score will remain as your best score unless you beat it in which itll keep changing until you die.
    and the lives tells you how many lives you have left, with it going down for each item missed.
